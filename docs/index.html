AI Usage Limits

How much of your Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Cursor and OpenRouter allowance is left — on your Home Screen.

Every provider shows your remaining quota somewhere, and it's somewhere different every time. This puts them on one screen and one widget.

No server. No account. No analytics.

You sign in on each provider's own page in Safari. Credentials stay in your device Keychain, and each token is sent only to the provider it belongs to. Nothing reaches the developer, because there is nothing to reach.
